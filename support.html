Frequently asked questions

How do I look up a vehicle?

Browse the offline catalog by make and model, or use search to ask in plain language — even with no signal.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The core catalog lives on your device. Online AI search and the EV Intel feed need a connection.

Is the app free?

The app is free for individual responders. Departments can license fleet-wide features — see licensing.

A vehicle is missing — can you add it?

Yes. Email us the make and model. Licensed departments can request priority coverage for their district.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@smartevguides.com with what happened and your device model.

Where can I download it?

On the App Store and Google Play.
