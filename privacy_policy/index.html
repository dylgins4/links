Health Data Disclaimer

This App provides informational reference guides about electric vehicle emergency response procedures (such as high-voltage safety and extrication). This content is general reference material intended for first responders.

This App does not:

  • Collect, access, store, process, or transmit any Health Data, medical data, biometric data, or fitness data from users
  • Access any device health sensors or health-related APIs (such as Google Health Connect, HealthKit, or similar services)
  • Function as a medical device, diagnostic tool, or health monitoring application
  • Provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations

The emergency vehicle reference content displayed in the App is static informational text and does not constitute Health Data as defined by Google Play's Data Safety policies.

Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device only to provide its core functionality:

  • Search preferences: Your most recently selected vehicle model, stored locally to improve your experience.
  • Downloaded content: Additional vehicle emergency guides downloaded from our servers for offline use.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Feedback Feature

The App includes an optional feedback feature that opens your device's default email client. If you choose to send feedback, the email draft may include basic diagnostic information (app version, device model, and operating system version) to help us troubleshoot issues. This information is only shared if you voluntarily choose to send the email. We do not automatically collect or transmit this data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect any data on our servers, there is no user data retained by us.

For locally stored data on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing app data: Go to your device Settings > Apps > Smart EV Guides > Storage > Clear Data. This removes all locally stored preferences and downloaded content.
  • Uninstalling the App: Removing the App from your device deletes all associated local data.

There is no account creation in this App, and therefore no account data to delete.

Network Usage

The App requires an internet connection only to download additional vehicle emergency guides that are not bundled with the initial installation. These downloads are direct file transfers and do not involve any user data being sent to our servers. No cookies, tokens, or tracking identifiers are transmitted.

Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection SDKs. The App does not share data with any third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.
