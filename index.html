“Built on the line,
not in a boardroom.”

Smart EV Guides was created by a working firefighter on Colorado's eastern corridor, out of a simple frustration: when an EV is involved, the answer shouldn't be buried in a 40-page PDF you can't open without signal. It's now used by hundreds of responders and sold to departments nationwide.
